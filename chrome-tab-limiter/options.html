Privacy Information

🔒 Local Storage Only

All your settings are stored locally on your device using Chrome's local storage. No data is ever sent to external servers or synced to Google accounts.

🔐 Privacy-Protected Exports

When you export settings, window and group IDs are anonymized to protect your browsing patterns. Only limit values are preserved.

🚫 No Data Collection

This extension does not collect, transmit, or store any personal browsing data. Tab titles and URLs are only used locally for the tab selection interface.
