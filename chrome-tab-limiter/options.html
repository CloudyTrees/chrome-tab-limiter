New Tabs Over the Limit

Max pending tabs per window

When a window is over its limit, new tabs stay open and wait for your decision. After this many, further new tabs are closed until you decide. Lowering this number never closes tabs that are already waiting.

Privacy Information

🔒 Local Storage Only

All your settings are stored locally on your device using Chrome's local storage. No data is ever sent to external servers or synced to Google accounts.

🔐 Privacy-Protected Exports

An export contains your limits, a count of how many you have set, the extension version, and numbered labels (window_1, group_1) that show which group limits belong to the same window. Real window and group IDs, tab titles and URLs are never included. Importing restores only the default limit and the pending-tab limit.

🚫 No Data Collection

This extension does not collect or transmit any data. Tab titles and URLs are read only to show the tab list in the popup and are never written to storage. Decisions in progress are kept in memory as window and tab IDs and counts.
